SURVEY RESEARCH HELP HUB — EXAMPLE FILES

These files are learning aids, NOT official LimeSurvey exports.

1. sustainable-marketing-questionnaire-build-template.csv: editable item-by-item questionnaire build plan. The six Likert rows are subquestions within two proposed parent arrays.
2. survey-test-checklist.csv: checks to complete before collecting real research responses.
3. sample-response-data.csv: FIVE ARTIFICIAL EXAMPLE RESPONSES, NOT RESEARCH DATA. Column names are illustrative; LimeSurvey exports may use different coding. Do not use for empirical results.

No .lss survey import file is supplied because it has not been tested against LimeSurvey CE 3.28.73+230906. To obtain a genuine .lss, build a survey in your own installation and use Display/Export > Survey structure (.lss). Validate by importing into a separate test survey.
